1. SYSTEM ACCESS & AUTHENTICATION POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Security Notice:
  Administrative and clinical access credentials are NOT hardcoded or stored in
  plaintext documentation. 
* Production Authentication:
  Authorized users must authenticate using their provisioned administrative
  or clinical accounts. Passwords must be verified via a secure backend
  authentication service with salted hashes (Argon2/bcrypt) and HTTP-only session
  tokens.
* Login Route:     http://localhost:5173/login?admin=1  (Shortcut: Alt + A)
* Dashboard URL:   http://localhost:5173/admin/dashboard


2. KEYBOARD SHORTCUT KEYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Alt + A]  (or Option + A on macOS)
  - Action:        Administrator Sign-In Mode Toggle
  - Scope:         Active globally across the entire website
  - Target Route:  /login?admin=1
  - Security:      Reveals and selects the Administrator login interface.
                   Credentials are NEVER pre-filled or bypassed. Authorized
                   administrators must enter their credentials manually.

[Ctrl + H]  (or Cmd + H on macOS)
  - Action:        Quick Register / Add New Hospital
  - Scope:         Active across the entire application and Admin Panel
  - Target Route:  /admin/hospitals?add=1
  - Behavior:      Opens the "Register New Hospital" modal with operational form
                   fields (hospital name, city, beds, ICU capacity, contact).

[Ctrl + D]  (or Cmd + D on macOS)
  - Action:        Hospital Partner Portal & Clinical Sign-In Modal
  - Scope:         Active across the entire application
  - Target Route:  /hospital/login  or  /hospital/portal
  - Behavior:      Opens the Hospital & Clinical Hub modal for clinical staff
                   access and hospital department management.


3. HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT PORTAL (/hospital/portal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Accessible via:
  - Route:       http://localhost:5173/hospital/portal
  - Login Page:  http://localhost:5173/hospital/login
  - Shortcut:    Press [Ctrl + D] from anywhere

Features & Operations:
  a) Add & Manage Doctors:
     - Register clinical doctors directly under the active hospital
     - Specify: Doctor Full Name, Specialization, Department, Qualifications,
       Experience Years, Consultation Fee (₹), Available Days & Hours,
       Phone, Email, and Clinical Bio
     - Doctors appear on the public doctors directory and hospital profile

  b) Add & Manage Wards & Bed Inventory ("New Ward and Details"):
     - Configure hospital wards and real-time inpatient capacity
     - Specify: Ward Name (e.g. ICU, ICCU, Pediatric Ward, Deluxe Private Suite, 
       Emergency Trauma Bay), Ward Category, Floor & Wing Location, Total Beds, 
       Available Beds, Daily Bed Charge (₹/Day), Head Nurse / In-Charge Name, 
       and Medical Equipment (Oxygen, Telemetry, Ventilator, etc.)
     - Real-time occupancy tracking with quick +/- 1 bed adjustment controls

  c) Hospital Infrastructure & Profile Details:
     - Update Hospital Name, Facility Type, Address, City, State
     - Official Phone, 24/7 Trauma Hotline, Email, Website
     - Total Inpatient Beds and ICU Capacity
     - Toggle 24/7 Emergency, Pharmacy, Ambulance Fleet, and Lab Services
     - Edit hospital description and NABH accreditation details
